03 / How an order moves

From cone to bale

One thread, five hands, from your indent to the lorry.

  1. 01

    Sourcing the cloth

    We buy from power-loom and handloom units across the Karur–Erode belt and match the quality to your brief: count, reed, pick and width.

  2. 02

    Checking the construction

    Before a metre moves, the sample is checked against the spec. Thread count, GSM and width are measured, not assumed.

  3. 03

    Processing

    Greige goes to partner process houses for whatever finish you need: bleached, RFD, dyed or printed.

  4. 04

    Inspection

    Fabric runs over the inspection table metre by metre. Faults are flagged and graded, and you get the report.

  5. 05

    Folding, baling, dispatch

    Folded or rolled, baled and marked, then sent by road anywhere in India with the paperwork done.

04 / Where we work

A town that weaves.

Karur sits on the banks of the Amaravathi and has woven cloth for generations. Today it is one of India's great home-textile towns, and its bed, kitchen and table linen goes to homes around the world.

Deer Fabrics works inside that network. We know the looms, the process houses and the people, so you get the right cloth without chasing ten suppliers.

We supply

  • aHome-textile exporters
  • bMade-up manufacturers
  • cGarment & stitching units
  • dWholesalers & retailers
  • eHotels, hospitals & institutions
